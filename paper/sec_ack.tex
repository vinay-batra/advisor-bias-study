\section*{Acknowledgments}
This paper was written with the assistance of a large language model, which
belongs to the same model family the paper measures. The analysis code, the
prose, and the reason-text labels of \movedref{app:reasons} were produced
that way, under the author's direction. The measurements were not: every
response came from the batch API under the seeds and identifiers recorded in
Section~\ref{sec:models}. The Ethics Statement sets out what that assistance
covers and the audit trail offered in place of a claim of independence. The
API credit was paid for by the author, and no external funding supported this
work.
