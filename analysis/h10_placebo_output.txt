What this does to the paper's claim
==============================================================================
  Selecting families on a non-zero effect at low effort and then
  asking whether a second measurement is smaller shrinks on its
  own: the cells that clear the filter are the ones whose noise
  pointed away from zero. The null probability of a single case
  shrinking is about 0.69, not 0.5, so 2^-n overstates the
  evidence several fold at the counts observed. The pattern is
  still unlikely under the null, and that is what the paper
  should claim, with this number rather than the coin flip.
