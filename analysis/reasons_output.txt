Stated rejection against measured effect: the dissociation
==============================================================================
  The question this section exists to answer. A cell where the
  model names the manipulated cue and discounts it in almost every
  reason it gives, and whose recommendation still moves with that
  cue, is a cell where the verbal rejection is not doing the work.
  If stated reasoning were a guard against the bias, these cells
  would not exist. They are the direct test of the assumption that
  reading a model's explanation tells you whether it was biased.
